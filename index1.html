The Problem

Post-Harvest Losses Stop Here

Traditional sun drying is unpredictable. Weather fluctuations, unexpected rain, and improper moisture levels lead to fungal growth, discoloration, and significant weight loss.

At Dhaanyaguah, we bridge the gap between traditional farming and modern technology. Our controlled drying units ensure uniform moisture reduction (12-14%), maximizing the market value of your paddy.

  • Prevention of Aflatoxin contamination
  • Uniform drying for every grain
  • Faster milling turnaround

Our Drying Solutions

Tailored drying methodologies for different scales of harvest.

Mechanical Batch Drying

High-capacity industrial dryers perfect for large cooperatives. Uses hot air recirculation to reduce moisture rapidly without cracking the grain.

Solar Hybrid Tunnel

Eco-friendly drying solution using solar collectors. Ideal for medium-scale farmers who want to reduce energy costs while maintaining quality.

Storage & Conditioning

Post-drying aeration services to keep paddy safe in silos. We monitor temperature and humidity to prevent spoilage during storage.
